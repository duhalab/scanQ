# Privacy Policy for ScanQ - QR & Barcode Scanner

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

Welcome to **ScanQ - QR & Barcode Scanner** ("we," "our," or "the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, usage, permissions, and local storage when you use our mobile application.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**ScanQ does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information to us or any third party.**

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use ScanQ.
- **On-Device Local Processing:** All QR and barcode scanning, generating, and history logging happen 100% locally on your device. We do not operate external servers to store your scanned data or generated codes.

If you manually enter personal information to generate a QR code (for example, to create a vCard or share contact details), that information remains on your device and is embedded only in the generated QR image unless you choose to share it yourself.

---

### 2. Permissions Used by the App

ScanQ requests only the minimum necessary permissions required to provide its core features. All data processing described below happens on-device unless explicitly stated otherwise.

1. **Camera Permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**
   - **Purpose:** Used exclusively to capture visual frames in real-time to detect and decode QR codes and barcodes.
   - **Data Handling:** Camera frames are analyzed locally in real-time on your device using on-device machine learning. Camera images are never recorded, uploaded, or transmitted anywhere.

2. **Photo / Gallery Access (System Photo Picker)**
   - **Purpose:** If you choose to scan a QR code from an existing image, the app uses Android's secure system Photo Picker.
   - **Data Handling:** Only the specific image you select is decoded locally. We do not access, browse, or read your photo library.

3. **Phone Call Permission (`android.permission.CALL_PHONE`)**
   - **Purpose:** If you scan a QR code containing a telephone number and choose the "Call" action, this permission allows you to place the call directly from the app.
   - **Data Handling:** The app does not access your call logs or contact books; it only initiates a call when you explicitly request it.

4. **Wi‑Fi State / Configure Permissions (`ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_STATE`, `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` when required by the OS for Wi‑Fi configuration)**
   - **Purpose:** If you scan a Wi‑Fi QR code and tap "Connect", the app can configure and connect to the designated Wi‑Fi network where platform APIs allow.
   - **Data Handling:** Wi‑Fi credentials entered or scanned are used only to connect (on-device) and are not transmitted to us.

5. **Vibration (`android.permission.VIBRATE`)**
   - **Purpose:** Provides subtle haptic feedback when a QR code or barcode is successfully decoded.

Note: ScanQ does not request unnecessary permissions such as broad Location, Contacts access (beyond what's needed for Wi‑Fi on some OS versions), Microphone, SMS‑reading, or background network access for analytics.

---

### 3. Local Data Storage

ScanQ stores the following information locally on your device only, using standard local storage (SQLite / SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to us or any third party unless you explicitly choose to share it.

| Data Type | Purpose | Where It's Stored |
|---|---|---|
| Scan History | Let you review previously scanned QR codes/barcodes | On-device only (local database) |
| Favorites | Let you mark and quickly access frequently used scans | On-device only |
| Generated QR Content | Content you enter to create QR codes (e.g. Wi‑Fi credentials, contact details, business card info, event details) | On-device only, and inside the QR image if you save or share it |
| App Settings | Language, theme, default flashlight/sound/gallery-scan preferences | On-device only |

You can clear this data at any time via **Settings → Clear History** within the App, or by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data.

---

### 4. QR Code Generation & Sensitive Information

When you use the "Create" feature to generate a QR code (for example, a Wi‑Fi network password, a vCard contact, a business card, or an event), the information you type is used solely to generate the QR and:

- Is not sent to ScanQ, Duha Lab, or any third‑party server;
- Remains on your device unless you choose to share or save the resulting QR image yourself;
- Is your responsibility to protect once shared — anyone who scans a shared QR code containing sensitive data (e.g. a Wi‑Fi password) will be able to read that information.

---

### 5. Third‑Party Services and Analytics

ScanQ is built with a privacy‑first approach:

- We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
- We do **NOT** use tracking cookies or invasive third‑party analytics SDKs that track you across apps or websites (for example, we do not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, AdMob, or similar services by default).
- Any web links (URLs) contained within scanned QR codes are opened only when you explicitly tap on them, using your default device web browser.

If a future version of the app includes third‑party services (for crash reporting, performance monitoring, or optional analytics), we will update this Privacy Policy in advance and clearly disclose the change.

### 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because ScanQ does not collect or transmit personal data to our servers, we do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. We have no user data to disclose to law enforcement or advertisers. If ever required by law to disclose data, we will comply with valid legal processes; however, since data is stored only on user devices, we do not possess user scan histories or generated content to provide.

### 7. Children's Privacy

ScanQ does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, it is designed to comply with COPPA and other applicable children's privacy protections.

### 8. Security

We value your trust. Because all data remains strictly on your local device and is never sent over the Internet to any proprietary backend by default, your data is protected by your device’s built‑in operating system security (screen lock, device encryption, secure storage). Please keep your device secure to protect locally stored scan history or generated codes.

### 10. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access:** All data ScanQ stores is directly visible to you within the App (History, Settings).
- **Deletion:** You can delete individual history items, clear all history, or uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.
- **Permission control:** You can revoke Camera, Photo/Gallery, or other permissions at any time via your device's system settings.

Because we do not hold any of your data on our servers, there is no dataset for us to export, transfer, or delete on your behalf — it already lives only on your device.
