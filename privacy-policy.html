Privacy Policy

App: ScanQ - QR & Barcode Scanner
Developer: Duha Lab
Effective Date: September 21, 2026
Last Updated: September 21, 2026
Summary: ScanQ does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or any third party. All scan history, generated QR codes, and settings are stored only on your own device. ScanQ does not require an internet connection, does not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not require account creation.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ScanQ - QR & Barcode Scanner ("ScanQ", "the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile and desktop application. We built ScanQ with privacy as a core design principle: the App works fully offline and does not send your data anywhere.

By using ScanQ, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

ScanQ does not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number) unless you manually enter it to generate a QR code, which stays on your device only
  • Location data
  • Device analytics or usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Any data sent to external servers, cloud storage, or third-party services

ScanQ does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any registration information.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ScanQ stores the following information locally on your device only, using standard local storage (SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to us or any third party:

Data TypePurposeWhere It's Stored
Scan HistoryLet you review previously scanned QR codes/barcodesOn-device only
FavoritesLet you mark and quickly access frequently used scansOn-device only
Generated QR ContentContent you enter to create QR codes (e.g. WiFi credentials, contact details, business card info, event details)On-device only, and inside the QR image if saved/shared
App SettingsLanguage, theme, default flashlight/sound/gallery-scan preferencesOn-device only

You can clear this data at any time via Settings → Clear History within the App, or by uninstalling the App, which removes all locally stored data.

4. Permissions We Request

PermissionWhy We Need It
CameraRequired to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera images are processed on-device only and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
Photos / StorageUsed only if you choose to scan a QR/barcode from an existing photo, or if you choose to save a generated QR code image to your gallery.
VibrationUsed to provide haptic feedback when a scan is successful.

ScanQ does not request Internet, Location, Contacts, Microphone, or SMS-reading permissions. You may grant or deny these permissions at any time through your device's system settings; denying camera access will limit the App's core scanning functionality.

5. QR Code Generation & Sensitive Information

When you use the "Create" feature to generate a QR code (for example, a WiFi network password, a vCard contact, a business card, or an event), the information you type is used solely to generate the QR code image on your device. This information:

  • Is not sent to ScanQ, Duha Lab, or any third-party server
  • Remains on your device unless you choose to share or save the resulting QR image yourself
  • Is your responsibility to protect once shared — anyone who scans a shared QR code containing sensitive data (e.g. a WiFi password) will be able to read that information

6. Third-Party Services

ScanQ does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The App does not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, AdMob, or similar services.

If a future version of the App includes any third-party service (for example, crash reporting or advertising), this Privacy Policy will be updated in advance, and the update will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because ScanQ does not collect or transmit personal data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any user data with third parties. We have no user data to disclose to law enforcement, advertisers, or any other party, as none is collected or stored outside your device.

8. Children's Privacy

ScanQ does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction), because the App does not collect personal data at all. The App is safe for general audiences.

9. Data Security

Since all data generated and stored by ScanQ remains on your own device, the security of that data depends on your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, physical access control). We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and using a secure lock screen.

10. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access: All data ScanQ stores is directly visible to you within the App (History, Settings).
  • Deletion: You can delete individual history items, clear all history, or uninstall the App at any time to remove all locally stored data.
  • Permission control: You can revoke Camera or Storage permissions at any time via your device's system settings.

Because we do not hold any of your data on our servers, there is no data for us to export, transfer, or delete on your behalf — it already lives only on your device, under your control.

11. International Users
