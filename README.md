# ScanQ — QR Code & Barcode Scanner

Download the app from Google Play:

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.duhalab.scanq

ScanQ is a fast, cross-platform Flutter app for scanning and generating QR codes and barcodes. It supports 16+ formats (QR, Data Matrix, Aztec, EAN, UPC, Code128, PDF417), works fully offline, and includes English and Bengali (বাংলা) language support — no ads, no tracking, no accounts.

## Features
- Scan QR codes and barcodes instantly
- Generate QR codes for text, URLs, contacts, Wi‑Fi, and more
- Works offline without an account
- Supports 16+ barcode and QR formats
- English and Bengali interface
